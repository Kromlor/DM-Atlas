# DM Atlas — user's spec (verbatim intent)

DM Atlas is an interactive Dungeon Master screen for the 2024 D&D 5e rules ("5.5e"). It helps a beginning DM learn the game and helps a DM run an adventure using PHYSICAL dice rolls entered into the app.

DELIVERY: one complete downloadable HTML file, embedded CSS/JS/reference data, runs locally in Chrome/Edge from file:// with no install, server, account, paid services, or external JS deps. Core features offline; external official rules links may need internet. No placeholders, TODO buttons, pseudocode.

APPEARANCE: fantasy DM-screen: dark wood framing, parchment panels, brass/gold accents, subtle purple highlights, large high-contrast text, decorative lettering for headings (system fonts only), practical lettering for instructions.
Main screen has four functional panels: 1 Guide the Game, 2 Rules & References, 3 Run Combat, 4 Session Tools. Include search, navigation, Full Screen, Monitor Setup, Pop Out This View, Save / Resume controls. Learning/table mode toggle. Mobile panel navigation.

THREE-MONITOR SUPPORT: separate browser windows (window.open with hash routes) for References, Combat, Party; also lessons, rulings, spell helper, events, NPCs, dice, notes, and full DM screen can pop out. Each window navigates independently. Party, combat, notes and logs sync between windows on same hosted origin & browser (BroadcastChannel + storage events). Do NOT promise sync for file:// URLs; explain the limitation in Monitor Setup (file:// origins may be treated as opaque/unique by Chrome; recommend a simple local host or same-window use; still attempt sync where it works). DM drags windows manually. No special multi-monitor API. Helpful instructions when pop-ups are blocked.

BEGINNER DM INSTRUCTION: 38 mini-lessons; a 12-lesson beginner sequence with practice questions and explanatory feedback; a five-scene guided first adventure. Cover: DM role and Describe -> Ask -> Resolve loop; preparing and starting a session; when a roll is necessary; ability checks, DCs, attacks, saving throws; initiative, turns, actions, movement, Bonus Actions, Reactions; spellcasting, cantrips, spell slots, components, Concentration; exploration, clues, NPC conversations, monsters, encounter preparation; sharing attention, improvising, recording consequences. Interactive "What Do I Roll?" decision guide: routine actions need no roll; impossible actions don't become possible via a high roll; specific rules take precedence over generic ability check. All lesson text must be original writing consistent with the 2024 rules / SRD 5.2.1 — do not invent rules.

REFERENCE LIBRARY: SRD 5.2.1 data: 339 spells incl. 27 cantrips; 38 weapons. Do not replace with a sample, mix older rules, or fabricate entries. Searchable spell, cantrip, weapon lists. Spell filters: level, class, Concentration. Spell cards: level, school, classes; casting time, range, components, duration; concentration and ritual; quick summary; DM resolution guidance; full spell effect and scaling ("Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot"/cantrip upgrade); official source links. Weapon cards: damage, damage type, properties, mastery, weight, cost, how to resolve attacks. SRD attribution (CC-BY-4.0) and license links.

PARTY DASHBOARD: create, edit, remove, backup export. Fields: character & player names; class/species and level; AC, current HP, max HP; passive Perception; spell save DC and spell attack bonus; initiative bonus; STR and DEX modifiers; simple and martial weapon proficiency; conditions; Concentration reminders; character notes; saved weapons.
PARTY CARDS FIT THE SCREEN: 4 characters -> 2x2 grid, 6 -> 3x2; resize on window/fullscreen change; no page scrolling for typical 4–6 desktop party; don't shrink text until unreadable; edit fields in a popup modal; show name, HP, AC, passive Perception, conditions, weapon buttons directly; two weapon buttons directly + "All Weapons" popup when more.

WEAPON SETUP & AUTO BONUSES: selecting a weapon fills damage dice, type, properties, mastery from reference. Versatile two-handed larger die. Default attack bonus and damage mod to 0 when ability info missing. STR for ordinary melee; DEX for ordinary ranged; Finesse: better of STR/DEX (allow choice); thrown follows weapon classification (melee weapon with Thrown uses STR unless Finesse). Proficiency Bonus from level (1-4:+2, 5-8:+3, 9-12:+4, 13-16:+5, 17-20:+6). Add PB to attack only when proficient (simple/martial category or specific-weapon proficiency). Normally no PB to damage. Manual overrides for magic weapons/special features kept SEPARATE from auto-calculated bonuses.

GUIDED WEAPON ATTACKS: each equipped weapon -> "Attack with [weapon]" button. Flow: 1 choose target from combatants or party; 2 show weapon info & bonuses; 3 fill known target AC, editable; 4 Advantage/Disadvantage; 5 enter physical d20 roll(s); 6 add attack bonus, show hit/miss/crit; 7 on hit show damage dice to roll; 8 accept sum of damage dice, add damage modifier once; 9 normal/resistance/vulnerability/immunity; 10 preview HP change; 11 apply only after DM confirmation; 12 record in encounter log.
Rules: meeting AC hits; nat 1 misses; nat 20 crits; adv & disadv cancel; crit doubles dice not flat mods (DM rolls twice the dice and enters the sum); resistance halves rounded down; vulnerability doubles; immunity prevents; no double apply; if target HP changed in another window, require fresh review; prompt Concentration save after damage when target is concentrating (DC = max(10, half damage), max 30); at 0 HP remind DM to resolve appropriate rules (death saves for PCs, etc.), never auto-kill.

COMBAT & SESSION TOOLS: combatant name, AC, Initiative, current/max HP, reminders, Concentration marker; initiative sort, round count, current turn, next/prev, HP adjustments; link party characters to combat (HP synced); encounter attack logs; dice roller with modifiers, adv/disadv, recent rolls; event generator (location, scene type, stakes, complications); NPC/captain generator (motives, traits, tactics, notes); session notebook with autosave and text export.

CAMPAIGN SAVE & JUMP DRIVE: End Night & Save, Export Campaign Save, Import Campaign Save, Resume Saved Session. Save: campaign name, party location, recap; party records & weapons; combatants, HP, initiative, round, current turn; conditions and notes; encounter logs; preserve unknown extra character fields. Use a versioned save format (e.g. {"app":"DM Atlas","format":"dm-atlas-campaign-save","version":...}); validate before replacing; show summary + confirmation; invalid saves must not corrupt current data. Portable workflow explained honestly (downloads go where the browser saves them; the user copies to the jump drive; local autosave doesn't travel with the HTML).

SCOPE: Don't claim future features. NOT implemented: full editable monster-sheet system, automated spell-effect engine, D&D Beyond import.

ADDITIONAL (from user in chat): import feature for campaigns (adventure content) and a campaign picker so the DM picks which campaign they're running.
