I. Your Letter Has Arrived

Read this aloud at your first session.

H

Dear Student,

It is our pleasure to inform you that the magic in you has been noticed. A place has been held for you at Hollowmere Academy, which sits on its island in the middle of the misty lake, as it has for a thousand years.

The sky-ferry leaves the river docks at dawn on the first day of autumn. Bring warm clothes, your own wand if you have one, and a curious mind. Leave behind any fear of the dark; you will not need it here.

On your first night you will stand in the light of the Founders' Lantern, and it will show you where you belong.

With warm regards,
Headmistress Aldra Fenwhistle
Hollowmere Academy

P.S. The East Wing is closed to all students. This is not a suggestion.

II. Hollowmere and Its Grounds

Hollowmere is an old castle on an island in a cold, misty lake. Students arrive by sky-ferry, an old flying riverboat pulled by a team of griffons.

🏮
Lantern Hall

The great dining hall. The Founders' Lantern hangs from the ceiling and glows in all four house colors at once.

📚
The Inkwell

The library, seven floors deep and built down into the island's rock. The bottom floors are for older students only.

🌿
The Glasshouses

Greenhouses full of plants that bite, sing, or wander off if you don't water them.

🔔
The Belltower

Its bell rings for classes, meals, and curfew. Star-reading lessons happen at the top.

🚪
The East Wing

Closed for as long as anyone remembers. The paintings in its halls are covered with sheets.

🌲
The Mirewood

A dark, boggy forest on the lake's north shore, full of creatures. Forbidden to students without a teacher.

🏘️
Fennick Cross

A small village on the south shore, reached by ferry-boat. Older students may visit on weekends for sweets, supplies, and gossip.

⛵
The Sky-Ferry

A flying riverboat pulled by griffons. It brings students in at the start of the year and home at the end.

III. The Four Houses

On your first night, you'll step into the light of the Founders' Lantern, and it will glow in your house's color. You choose your house; the Lantern just makes it official. Your house is your family at Hollowmere: you sleep in its tower, eat at its table, and earn points for it all year.

🦎

House Emberly

Founded by Brannoch Emberly, who stood alone on the castle walls the night the lake froze. Emberly students rush in first and ask questions later.

IV. Making Your Student

Hollowmere uses the normal D&D 2024 Player's Handbook rules. You build your character the usual way, then add three Hollowmere pieces: a house, a wand, and bonds with your classmates. Everyone starts at level 1 as a first-year student.

Choose your Study Path (class)

Every student at Hollowmere has magic, but it shows up in different ways. Pick any class and use its Hollowmere name below. Your class still works exactly as the rulebook says.

Choose your Origin (background and species)

Your background is your life before Hollowmere; it gives you your ability score increases and an Origin feat. Most students are human, but elves, dwarves, halflings, gnomes, and the other species in the rulebook all attend too.

Set your ability scores

Use the standard array (15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8) or whatever method your DM chooses, then add your background's increases.

Choose your House

Pick Emberly, Tidewell, Thornwood, or Duskmoor. Write down your house skill and house trait.

Roll your Wand

Roll for your wand's wood and core on the tables below, or pick the ones you like.

Pack your school trunk

Take your class's normal starting equipment, plus a free Hollowmere kit: a school robe in your house colors, two textbooks, ink and a quill, a small cauldron, and a candle lantern.

Make two bonds

At the first session, answer one icebreaker question, then make a bond with the player to your left, such as met on the docks, cousins, rivals from home, or sat together on the ferry.

Your character sheet: print the First-Year Character Sheet (two pages, tinted in your house color) and fill it in with pencil as you go through these steps.

Skills · Dexterity
The Wand-Blade Rule. Students who fight with weapons can describe their attacks as magic from their wand. A Duelist's longsword might be a "cutting hex," and a Beastwarden's bow might be a "bolt of light." Only the description changes: the attack uses the same ability score, damage dice, damage type, range, and Weapon Mastery as the real weapon. It is still a weapon attack, not a spell, so it needs no spell slots. You can also carry an ordinary sword or bow if you'd rather.
Wands as Focus. At Hollowmere, any spellcaster can use their wand as their spellcasting focus, whatever their class.

IX. Life at Hollowmere

Between adventures, you're still a student. These rules make the school year part of the game.

🏆 House Points

Teachers give and take points all year.

  • Earn 5–20 for clever ideas, brave acts, kindness, or winning a class challenge.
  • Lose 5–20 for breaking curfew, fighting in the halls, or getting caught where you shouldn't be.
  • At year's end, the house with the most points wins the Lantern Cup. Every member of the winning house starts next year with Heroic Inspiration.

📝 Classes & Exams

A class is a quick skill challenge: make three checks. Two or three successes earns a good grade and points.

  • Charms: Arcana
  • Potions: Medicine or Intelligence
  • Creature Care: Animal Handling
  • Greenlore: Nature
  • Hollowmere History: History
  • Broomwork: Acrobatics or Athletics

Typical DC: 10 easy, 13 normal, 15 hard.

🌙 Curfew & Detention

Students must be in their house tower when the Belltower rings ten. Get caught out and you'll earn detention: an evening scrubbing cauldrons, sorting the Inkwell, or helping the caretaker. Detention often turns up clues.

🕯️ Downtime

Between adventures, each student picks one activity:

  • Study: Advantage on one check in the next adventure tied to that subject.
  • Join a club: dueling, choir, star-reading, or broomwork.
  • Work in Fennick Cross: earn a little gold.
  • Explore the castle: the DM may reveal a secret.

X. Growing Up at Hollowmere

The campaign follows your class from first year to graduation. Each year you get a new, more advanced character sheet that keeps everything from the last one and adds a new section.

YearLevelsNew on your sheet
First-Year1–3The basics: house, wand, first spells, classmate bonds. At level 3, choose your Path (subclass).
Second-Year4–5Electives: feats shown as school courses; stronger spells
Third-Year6–7Familiar or companion; secret society membership
Fourth-Year8–10Tournament record; a signature spell
Fifth-Year11–12Exam results that unlock a mastery perk
Sixth-Year13–14Apprenticeship with a mentor
Graduate15–17Legacy feature and your diploma, just in time for the final battle
Something to keep in mind: Hollowmere has kept its secrets for a thousand years. Some of them are about to wake up. Watch the paintings.
